# Urchinbot — Veo & Image Gen Prompt Ideas

Use urchin.png as a reference/starting image for these prompts.
Character: round purple sea urchin, chibi/kawaii style, dark purple body with lighter lavender face, sharp conical spines (some with yellow glow tips), large dark blue eyes with white sparkle highlights, small upturned smile, pink blush cheeks, white stubby feet, floating blue bubbles, pink starfish left, golden star right, light blue oval base.

────────────────────────────────────────────
VEO / VIDEO PROMPTS (image-to-video starters)
────────────────────────────────────────────

1. The urchin mascot slowly blinks, bubbles drift upward, soft underwater light. 5 sec, calm, whimsical.

2. Gentle bounce or wiggle — urchin bounces once, bubbles and stars shimmer. Fluid animation, kawaii style. 3–4 sec.

3. Awakening — start from still, eyes gain sparkle, pink star rotates slowly, background fades from black to soft underwater glow. 5 sec.

4. Zoom out — urchin in frame, camera pulls back to reveal coral reef, friendly fish swim past. Same art style. 6 sec.

5. Thinking pose — urchin tilts, thought bubble appears with a small star. 4 sec.

6. Happy reaction — urchin bounces twice, eyes sparkle more, smile widens. 3 sec.

7. Floating — urchin drifts slowly upward through blue water, bubbles rise around it. 5 sec, dreamy.

8. Waving — one stubby foot waves, urchin rotates slightly. 3 sec, loop-friendly.

9. Code mode — urchin surrounded by floating code brackets and symbols, spins slowly. Tech/product vibe. 5 sec.

10. Token scan — urchin holds tiny magnifying glass, inspects floating token icon, nods. 5 sec, crypto theme.

• Use #00FF00 or #00B140 for best keying — avoid green in subject (urchin is purple, safe)
• Request "evenly lit" and "no shadows on background" to reduce keying artifacts
• "No green spill" keeps edges clean for compositing
